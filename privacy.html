Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how this game collects, uses and protects your information.

Information We Collect

The game may store information such as:

Artificial Intelligence

This game uses an AI language model that runs locally on your device. Story generation is performed offline whenever possible.

Your story content is not transmitted to us for AI generation.

Downloads

The application downloads an AI model after your permission. This model is required for AI-generated storytelling.

Analytics

If analytics are used in future versions, they may collect anonymous usage information to improve the game.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed through the relevant app store. We do not receive your payment information.

Children

The game supports different age settings and attempts to generate age-appropriate content. Parents or guardians remain responsible for supervising younger players.

Data Storage

Story data and settings are primarily stored on your device.

Your Choices

You may:
