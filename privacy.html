Living Tales - Privacy Policy

Version: 1

Effective Date: July 2026

Overview

Living Tales is a mobile narrative role-playing game. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled within the application.

Data Storage

Living Tales does not use a backend server. All user data is stored locally on your device only.

  • Profiles
  • Story progress
  • Settings (audio, language, gameplay preferences)

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly transfer or back it up yourself.

Data Collection

Living Tales does not directly collect personal data such as name, email, or location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game may display advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect limited data to provide personalized or non-personalized ads.

For more information, see: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

In-App Purchases

Living Tales uses Google Play Billing and Apple In-App Purchases for premium features and subscriptions. Payment data is handled securely by Google and Apple. The game itself does not store payment details.

Analytics

The game does not use third-party analytics services.

Children’s Privacy

The game may be accessible to younger users. No personal data is collected by the application.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in future versions of the game. Users are encouraged to review it periodically.

Contact

If you have any questions, you may contact the developer through the official store listing or support channel.
